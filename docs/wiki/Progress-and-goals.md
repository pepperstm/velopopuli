# Progress and goals

![Progress](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pepperstm/velopopuli/main/docs/images/screen-progress.jpg)

- **Level and XP** for every kilometre and climb, and **badges** for milestones.
- **Season goal** — 1,000 km between 1 September and 20 March by default (change it in Settings), with the distance per week and **week and day streaks**.
- **Power curve** from 5 seconds to an hour, over the last six weeks or all time.
- **FTP estimate** from your best efforts, offered after a ride when it beats the one set — one click to use it.
- **Training load** — fitness, fatigue and form, from each ride's TSS.
- **Power zones** on the HUD.
