NEW IN v0.3.5 / BLEEDING EDGE

Leave the tarmac.
Help shape the ride.

Mountain-bike trails, waymarked MTB routes and rough-surface feel are new in v0.3.5. Try them now and help us tune how they ride on real trainers.

01 / MOUNTAIN-BIKE TRAILS

Take the singletrack.

Ride narrow earth trails without crowd barriers. Mapped MTB grades appear on the HUD, with harder grades adding rolling resistance to the virtual ride and trainer load. Bank into berms on tight turns, cross streams on log bridges and splash through fords. Trail detail depends on the available OpenStreetMap data.

02 / WAYMARKED MTB ROUTES

Find a trail worth following.

In the app’s route finder, plan along trails or choose from the waymarked MTB routes mapped in the area on screen. Discover a route, then bring it indoors.

03 / ROUGH-SURFACE FEEL

Feel the extra work.

Gravel, cobbles and earth add rolling resistance, affecting virtual speed and adding load through your trainer. Surface feel is optional: switch it off in Settings → Trainer & physics → Feel the road surface. The resistance balance across surfaces and MTB grades is still being tuned.

New, experimental and improving. Real-trainer feedback is helping tune these features. Ride a route you know and tell us your trainer model, the surface or MTB grade, and what felt right or wrong.

02 / CONNECT

Let your trainer follow the road.

Set your smart trainer to ERG or Gradient mode over Bluetooth FTMS, Tacx FE-C or ANT+ FE-C. Power, heart-rate, speed and cadence sensors are supported too. Prefer not to shift your bike? Optional virtual gears let you change gear with keys, a Bluetooth remote or on-screen buttons.

03 / PROGRESS

Race your previous best.

Ride against one of your past efforts, try to catch another rider or settle in behind a pace rider. Timed climbs give you a target; XP, levels and 23 badges mark the small wins. Track power curves, training load and FTP estimates in the dashboard.

Will VeloPopuli work with my setup?+

The app runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. It can control trainers over Bluetooth FTMS, Tacx FE-C and ANT+ FE-C, and read standard power, heart-rate, speed and cadence sensors.

Trainer and sensor support can vary by model and computer. Check the project notes for what has been tested so far.

Beyond the new trails, VeloPopuli gives you ways to train with purpose, chase a better time and share the road.

01 / WORKOUTS ON ANY ROUTE

Your workout. Your favourite climb.

Choose a built-in session or import a .zwo, .erg or .mrc workout. Endurance, sweet spot, intervals or an FTP ramp test: ERG mode targets your power while the route rolls by. Adjust intensity or skip a step mid-ride.

Train on your terms ↗
02 / GHOSTS & PACE RIDERS

Give yourself someone to catch.

Race the ghost of your best ride, chase a pace rider at a steady W/kg or take on a timed climb. See whether you’re ahead or behind your best, then follow your power curve, training load and FTP estimate as you improve.

Bring your riding friends.

Share a room code and ride the same route together, with everyone’s rider in the world. Group rides currently need a reachable relay server; you or your club can host the small open-source relay yourselves.

05 / HELP BUILD IT

Could you help us build it?

Help tune v0.3.5’s new trails and rough-surface feel on real trainers.

Real trainer and sensor testing is only just beginning, and Windows and Linux have had little real-world use. Try it with your setup and tell us what worked, what broke and what you’d like to see next.

A bug report, a feature idea or a bit of code all help. If you don’t code, you can still help by improving the setup guide.
