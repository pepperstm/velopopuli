      Before you export

      Distances, climbs and gradients are estimates based on the roads and elevation data available in your area. Check the map and profile before you export.

      This planner is designed for indoor rides. If you ride the route outside, check local access, road surfaces and conditions first.

      Privacy and map credits

      Place searches go to Photon, and route requests go to BRouter through this website. We cache routes for up to seven days and refresh them after a day; requests may be briefly rate-limited to keep the service available. Imported GPX files are processed in your browser. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. We don’t create an account or save a route history.
